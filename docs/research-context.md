# Research context

I built ConfusionMapper as the reliability check for a preregistered RCT in government junior high schools in Kanpur Dehat, Uttar Pradesh (OSF: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/YU6P5). I am the sole researcher on the study, working as an independent researcher from Kanpur.

The RCT asks whether telling a student what *kind* of mistake they made (recall failure, partial knowledge, confabulation, interference) produces better retention two weeks later than plain correctness-only feedback.

The study's preregistration says we cannot collect any real data until two coders can label the same distractors with Cohen's kappa >= 0.70. This package is that gate. It was written before participant recruitment began, precisely so the taxonomy could not be shaped by data collected under it.

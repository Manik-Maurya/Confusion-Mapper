Maths in real life

Free PISA-style maths worksheets set in situations Indian children know: phone data plans, the monsoon, the price of dal, the bus to the district headquarters. Every wrong answer in the teacher's key says what a child who chose it probably believes, and after the test the class report tells you what to reteach first.
